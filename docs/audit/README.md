# External audits

Read-only reviews of the record by someone outside the effort, kept beside the spec rather than
inside it: nothing here is an ADR, a register or a ruling, and nothing here binds. Where an audit
disagrees with a decision, the decision is the record and the audit is why it looked worth
re-reading.
